GroundTwin Scout

Type any place on Earth or coordinates. Scout runs our Analog Engine formula on the spot — it is not a fixed list — and tells you how well that place matches a Moon base site or a Mars landing site. Ask about a lunar south-pole crater to see its ice evidence. Scout is not a chatbot that makes things up: every number is computed from our data.

Match % = weighted similarity on our 7 criteria (S × 100). It measures similarity, not whether a base can be built there. Rank is among all … land cells (…° grid, Antarctica excluded). Place names: our data files first, otherwise OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding.

Data: NASA GPM IMERG, MODIS NDVI (MOD13A2), LST (MOD11A2), aerosol (MOD08_M3), USGS GMTED2010; NASA LRO Mini-RF, Diviner, LOLA (PDS). Geocoding © OpenStreetMap contributors (Nominatim). Not endorsed by NASA.
